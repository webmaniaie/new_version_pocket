The first two seconds.

HooksRetentionScripting

The problem

Nobody chose your reel.

A viewer did not open your video the way they open a film. It arrived. The only decision they make is whether to keep holding still, and they make it in about two seconds — before they know the topic, the brand or the point.

So the hook is not the intro. It is the whole negotiation. Everything after it only matters to the people it kept.

What a hook has to do

Three jobs, two seconds.

01 Make a promise worth waiting for
02 Show the payoff exists
03 Leave one thing unresolved

Four that keep working

01 The wrong way first Show the mistake, then the fix ↗︎
02 Mid-action open Start at the interesting second, not the first ↗︎
03 Named audience Say exactly who this is for, out loud ↗︎
04 Visible stake Something on screen can still go wrong ↗︎

Testing one

You do not need to reshoot to test a hook. Re-cut the same reel with a different first two seconds and post it a week apart. Compare the retention graph, not the likes: if the new opening holds people past the three second mark, keep it and move on to the next reel. One variable at a time, or you learn nothing.
