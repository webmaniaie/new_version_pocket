## [Ireland agency guide] Which short-form video agency is right for your brand?

The best **short-form video agencies in Ireland** do more than crop a horizontal advert into 9:16. They understand hooks, retention, native platform behaviour, repeatable formats and the difference between an organic Reel and a paid-social creative.

This guide compares ten agencies with an Irish base or a clear Irish operation and a public service offering relevant to Instagram Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts. It is designed for businesses choosing between a focused production partner and a broader social media agency.

**Disclosure:** Reels Agency publishes this article and is ranked second. This is not an independent awards list. We applied the same public criteria to every agency, linked the sources used and have clearly stated where our own offer is strong and where a larger competitor has an advantage.

## [How we ranked them] A practical buyer-focused methodology {white}

The ranking was researched on 4 August 2026 using agency websites, published service pages, portfolios and public case evidence. We did not request private pitch decks, unpublished prices or client analytics.

| Factor | Weight | What we looked for |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form relevance | 30% | Reels, TikTok and Shorts treated as native formats rather than an afterthought |
| Public evidence | 25% | Visible work, named clients, case studies or documented performance |
| Service completeness | 20% | Strategy, concepts, scripting, filming, editing, delivery and optional distribution |
| Clarity and transparency | 15% | A buyer can understand the offer, process, location and—where published—pricing |
| Irish accessibility | 10% | An Irish office, local production access or a clearly stated Ireland-based operation |

Scores are editorial estimates based only on public evidence. They are a comparison tool, not an audited certification. A specialist may be the best choice for one brief even when a larger full-service agency ranks higher overall.

## [Agency notes] What each option does best {white}

### 1. Simply Social: strongest full social-first offer

Simply Social describes itself as a social-first creative agency and publicly lists Dublin and London offices. Its service mix covers social strategy, content, influencer campaigns, creator-led and UGC production, paid campaigns and performance analysis. That breadth makes it the strongest all-round option in this review for brands that need more than video production.

### 2. Reels Agency: strongest focused value proposition

Reels Agency is built around one output: vertical short-form video. The service covers ideas, scenarios, filming, editing and posting, with one-off and monthly starting prices published on the [pricing page](/product). Its public Sweet Baking Mama case documents more than 750K views on one Reel and 1,600+ combined follower growth over three months.

The trade-off is scale. The agency is newer, has a smaller portfolio and should be assessed through a pilot pack or clear monthly brief. For owner-led businesses in Carlow, Dublin and surrounding counties, the lean setup may also mean faster access to the people actually creating the work.

### 3. Radiant Creative Social: strong creative and creator network

Radiant combines senior strategy with lo-fi, reactive social content and higher-production campaign assets. Its public service page also covers account management, influencer partnerships and paid social, making it suitable for brands that want cultural insight and a creator network around the production work.

### 4. Way Out West: strong for food, beverage and DTC creative

Way Out West is a Dublin creative marketing and video production agency with a public focus on paid growth, social-first creative and influencer storytelling. Its founder-led ads, UGC and food-and-beverage positioning make it a particularly relevant shortlist choice for consumer brands.

### 5. likes.ie: strong for end-to-end social management

likes.ie positions its work around platform-native content rather than copying one asset across TikTok, Reels and Shorts. It combines production with strategy, account management, paid social and influencer campaigns, which suits businesses that want the agency to run the wider channel rather than only deliver videos.

### 6. NextVision Studio: short-form plus broader visual production

NextVision Studio publicly offers Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts, photography, brand video, podcast content and social ad creative. It is a useful option for Dublin businesses that want one production supplier for both everyday social content and wider brand assets.

### 7. Pluto: studio-backed social-first content

Pluto describes an in-house social-first content unit producing native work for paid and organic channels, both in its studio and on location. Its broader advertising and communications capability may suit larger organisations that need short-form assets within an integrated campaign.

### 8. Titan Digital: practical ongoing content for SMEs

Titan Digital offers social content creation to businesses across Ireland and explicitly names Reels, TikToks and Shorts. Its positioning connects the videos to business goals and ongoing brand visibility, which can suit an SME looking for regular marketing support.

### 9. Apex Vertical: direct access to a solo specialist

Apex Vertical is an Ireland-based solo short-form agency offering vertical editing, content strategy and social media management. The direct-contact model may appeal to brands that prefer one operator and a simple workflow. Buyers should ask for recent named case studies and delivery capacity during the proposal stage.

### 10. Creo Media: broad visual-media partner

Creo Media is a Dublin visual media agency offering script-to-screen production alongside brand guidelines and web design. Short-form video is part of a wider production service, making it more relevant when a business also needs broader visual assets rather than a dedicated Reels programme alone.

## [How to choose] Questions to ask before signing {white}

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Who develops the hooks and recurring formats? | Editing cannot rescue a content idea that gives viewers no reason to stay |
| Is filming included, and where will it happen? | Travel, studio, talent and location costs can materially change the quote |
| How many finished videos and revisions are included? | “Monthly content” can mean very different volumes and approval processes |
| Are organic posting, paid usage and raw files included? | Usage rights and distribution should be agreed before production begins |
| Which result will be reported? | Views alone are not enough; ask about retention, qualified reach, enquiries or sales |
| Can we begin with a pilot? | A smaller first project tests communication, quality and brand fit before a longer contract |
