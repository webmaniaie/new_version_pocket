Product 01

Reels Sprint

One brief, one pack of reels, one delivery. You get a set of finished vertical videos ready to post. Best for a launch, a season, or a first test of short-form before committing to a monthly rhythm.

Price
From €500 for the pack. Final quote depends on how many reels and how much filming we do.
Timing
2–3 weeks from the kick-off call to delivery.
Format
9:16, sound-on, captioned, exported ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Product 03

Symbiosis

For brands that already have a camera, a face or a studio. We split the pipeline: we bring the ideas, write the scenarios and edit them, you shoot the raw footage — or we flip it the other way around, whichever way your team is strongest.

Book this ↗︎ See the work

This one is genuinely a conversation. Tell us your setup and we'll shape it.

Price
Set per split — the more you take on, the lower it goes. Quoted after a call.
Timing
Ongoing, with a delivery rhythm we agree up front.
Split
Any mix of ideas, scenarios, filming, editing and posting.
  • A call to map who does what
  • Ideas and written scenarios from us
  • Raw footage from you, to an agreed shot list
  • Editing, captions and exports from us
  • Or the reverse split — you edit, we produce and direct
